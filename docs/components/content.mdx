Everything that shows something: text, pictures, icons, shapes, lists and progress.

<Callout type="info">
  Building a long list out of a Column creates one component per item. Use **RecyclerView** instead —
  it builds a handful of rows and reuses them as they scroll.
</Callout>

| Component | Does |
|---|---|
| [TextView](#textview) | Displays a piece of read-only text, from a one-word label to a paragraph, with control over font, size, color, alignment and truncation. |
| [Image](#image) | A picture drawn from an uploaded asset, with control over how it fills its frame plus rounded corners, a border and a shadow. |
| [Icon](#icon) | A single square icon picked from the built-in icon set, tinted and sized from the properties panel. |
| [Shape](#shape) | A vector shape — a rectangle, a circle, one of the expressive shapes such as a cookie, a clover or an arch, or a custom outline — drawn with a fill and an outline. |
| [RecyclerView](#recyclerview) | A scrollable list or grid whose row views are built once and reused as they scroll, each row filled in by the Bind Item event. |
| [ChatView](#chatview) | A whole conversation in one component: message bubbles with times, ticks, avatars, names and date separators, a typing line, and a composer with a send button. |
| [GraphView](#graphview) | Draws a line, bar or pie chart from a list of labelled values, and reports the point, bar or slice the user tapped. |
| [ProgressBar](#progressbar) | A horizontal bar that fills to show how far along a task is, either at a set percentage or as an endless animation while the length is unknown. |
| [CircularProgress](#circularprogress) | A round progress ring that either spins to show the app is busy or fills up to show how far a task has got. |
| [Notifier](#notifier) | A non-visual helper that shows brief toast messages and alert, confirm and popup dialogs on top of the current screen. |

## TextView

Displays a piece of read-only text, from a one-word label to a paragraph, with control over font, size, color, alignment and truncation.

*Drawn on the screen.*

## Image

A picture drawn from an uploaded asset, with control over how it fills its frame plus rounded corners, a border and a shadow. It also reports taps.

*Drawn on the screen.*

**Events** — `Click`, `Long Press`, `Load`, `Error`

## Icon

A single square icon picked from the built-in icon set, tinted and sized from the properties panel. Used beside labels, inside buttons and in navigation rows.

*Drawn on the screen.*

## Shape

A vector shape — a rectangle, a circle, one of the expressive shapes such as a cookie, a clover or an arch, or a custom outline — drawn with a fill and an outline. Children placed inside are clipped to it.

*Drawn on the screen.*

**Events** — `Click`, `Long Press`

## RecyclerView

A scrollable list or grid whose row views are built once and reused as they scroll, each row filled in by the Bind Item event.

*Drawn on the screen.*

**Events** — `Bind Item`, `Refresh`, `Scroll`

**Functions** — `Refresh Data`, `Scroll To Position`, `Get Row Component`

## ChatView

A whole conversation in one component: a scrolling list of message bubbles — yours on the
right, theirs on the left — with times, delivery ticks, avatars, names, date separators and
a typing line, and a composer at the bottom with a send button and an optional attach
button. The list keeps its place when the keyboard opens, and stays pinned to the newest
message when the reader was already there. See [Build a chat app](/guides/apps/chat-app).

*Drawn on the screen.*

**Events** — `Send`, `Attach`, `Message Click`, `Message Long Press`, `Reached Top`, `Typing`

**Functions** — `Scroll To Bottom`, `Add Message`, `Update Message`, `Clear`

### A message

**Messages** is a list of records, one per message. Setting it redraws the list — pinned to
the bottom if the reader was there, else where they were. Each record is a dictionary:

| Key | Holds |
|---|---|
| `id` | Text that identifies the message — what `Message Click`, `Message Long Press` and `Update Message` name it by. |
| `text` | The message, or a picture's caption. |
| `senderId`, `senderName` | Who sent it. A `senderId` equal to **My Id** draws on the right. |
| `avatar` | An image URL, one or two initials, or `""` — drawn beside the bubble when **Show Avatars** is on. |
| `time` | Display text such as `10:42`, under the last bubble of a run when **Show Timestamps** is on. |
| `date` | A day label such as `Today` or `12 Mar`. When it differs from the previous message's, a centred separator is drawn; `""` draws none. |
| `status` | `sending`, `sent`, `delivered`, `read`, `failed` or `""`. On your own messages, with **Show Status** on, it draws a clock, one tick, two ticks, two ticks in the accent, or a red mark. |
| `imageUrl` | A picture drawn above the text, or `""`. |

Consecutive messages from one sender form a run, drawn close together, with the avatar
beside the last bubble and the name above the first. A bubble takes at most 78% of the
width.

### Properties

| Property | Does |
|---|---|
| **Messages**, **My Id** | The list above, and the sender id that counts as you. |
| **My Bubble Color**, **Their Bubble Color**, **My Text Color**, **Their Text Color**, **Time Color** | The two bubble fills and their text, and the colour of times, names, the typing line and the empty state. The defaults follow the theme: primary and on-primary for yours, sheet and on-background for theirs, neutral for times. |
| **Bubble Radius** | The corner radius in dp (18). The corner nearest the sender tightens on the last bubble of a run. |
| **Font**, **Font Size** | As on TextView (16 sp). |
| **Show Avatars**, **Show Names** | Off by default; turn both on for a group chat. A name is never drawn on your own messages. |
| **Show Timestamps**, **Show Status** | On by default. |
| **Typing Text** | `Priya is typing…` draws a typing line with animated dots under the list; `""` hides it. |
| **Empty Text** | Centred in the list when there are no messages (`Say hello`). |
| **Show Composer**, **Placeholder**, **Send Icon**, **Attach Icon** | The composer (on), its placeholder (`Message`), the send button's icon (`Send`), and the attach button's icon — `""` means no attach button. |
| **Composer Text** | What is typed. Read it to send a picture with a caption; set it to `""` to clear. |

### Events and functions

- **Send** (text) — the send button or the keyboard's send key, with the trimmed text, once it is not empty. The composer has already cleared itself.
- **Attach** — the attach button.
- **Message Click** and **Message Long Press** (id).
- **Reached Top** — the list reached its first message: load older ones.
- **Typing** (typing) — true when the composer goes from empty to text, false two seconds after the last keystroke or when it empties.
- **Add Message** (record) — appends one message and scrolls to it: the optimistic send.
- **Update Message** (id, record) — replaces only the fields given, such as a status change.
- **Scroll To Bottom** and **Clear**.

## GraphView

Draws a line, bar or pie chart from a list of labelled values, and reports the point, bar or slice the user tapped.

*Drawn on the screen.*

**Events** — `Value Selected`

**Functions** — `Set Data`, `Clear Data`, `Set Graph Type`

## ProgressBar

A horizontal bar that fills to show how far along a task is, either at a set percentage or as an endless animation while the length is unknown.

*Drawn on the screen.*

**Events** — `Click`

## CircularProgress

A round progress ring that either spins to show the app is busy or fills up to show how far a task has got.

*Drawn on the screen.*

## Notifier

A non-visual helper that shows brief toast messages and alert, confirm and popup dialogs on top of the current screen.

*Draws nothing — drop it anywhere on the screen and it contributes blocks only.*

**Events** — `Alert Closed`, `Confirm Selected`, `Popup Closed`

**Functions** — `Show Toast Default`, `Show Toast`, `Show Alert`, `Show Confirm`, `Show Popup`
