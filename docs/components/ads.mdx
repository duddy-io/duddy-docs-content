Banner, interstitial and rewarded placements from AdMob and Meta Audience Network.

<Callout type="info">
  Ad network IDs are project-level and set once under
  [Settings → Integrations](/getting-started/project-settings#integrations). Every ad component carries
  a **Test Mode** wired to the network's real test units — leave it on until you ship.
</Callout>

| Component | Does |
|---|---|
| [AdMobBanner](#admobbanner) | A Google AdMob banner ad strip, sized entirely by its Ad Size and usually pinned along the top or bottom of a screen. |
| [AdMobInterstitial](#admobinterstitial) | A non-visual Google AdMob full-screen ad, loaded in advance and shown at a natural break such as the end of a level or an article. |
| [AdMobRewarded](#admobrewarded) | A non-visual rewarded video ad. |
| [FacebookBanner](#facebookbanner) | A banner ad served by Meta Audience Network, sized by its Ad Size and refreshed with Load Ad. |
| [FacebookInterstitial](#facebookinterstitial) | A full-screen Meta Audience Network ad shown at natural breaks in an app, such as between screens or after a game level. |

## AdMobBanner

A Google AdMob banner ad strip, sized entirely by its Ad Size and usually pinned along the top or bottom of a screen.

*Drawn on the screen.*

**Events** — `Ad Loaded`, `Ad Failed To Load`, `Ad Opened`, `Ad Closed`, `Ad Clicked`

**Functions** — `Load Ad`

**Android permissions** — `INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`

## AdMobInterstitial

A non-visual Google AdMob full-screen ad, loaded in advance and shown at a natural break such as the end of a level or an article.

*Draws nothing — drop it anywhere on the screen and it contributes blocks only.*

**Events** — `Ad Loaded`, `Ad Failed To Load`, `Ad Opened`, `Ad Closed`, `Ad Clicked`

**Functions** — `Load Ad`, `Show Ad`, `Is Loaded`

**Android permissions** — `INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`

## AdMobRewarded

A non-visual rewarded video ad. The reward event fires only when genuinely earned, never on dismissal.

*Draws nothing — drop it anywhere on the screen and it contributes blocks only.*

**Events** — `Ad Loaded`, `Ad Failed To Load`, `Ad Shown`, `Ad Dismissed`, `Reward Earned`

**Functions** — `Is Loaded`, `Load Ad`, `Show Ad`

## FacebookBanner

A banner ad served by Meta Audience Network, sized by its Ad Size and refreshed with Load Ad. Requires a placement id from Meta Monetization Manager.

*Drawn on the screen.*

**Events** — `Ad Loaded`, `Ad Failed To Load`, `Ad Opened`, `Ad Clicked`

**Functions** — `Load Ad`

**Android permissions** — `INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`

## FacebookInterstitial

A full-screen Meta Audience Network ad shown at natural breaks in an app, such as between screens or after a game level.

*Draws nothing — drop it anywhere on the screen and it contributes blocks only.*

**Events** — `Ad Loaded`, `Ad Failed To Load`, `Ad Opened`, `Ad Closed`, `Ad Clicked`

**Functions** — `Load Ad`, `Show Ad`, `Is Loaded`

**Android permissions** — `INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`
