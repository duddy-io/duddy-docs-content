Containers that decide where everything else goes — plus the bars, tabs and overlays a screen is framed by.

<Callout type="info">
  Sizing behaves differently inside a scrolling container: on the scrolling axis, **Fill** means
  "as tall as the content". See [Layout and sizing](/guides/designing/layout-and-sizing).
</Callout>

## Screen

The root of every screen in an app: it holds the components placed on it and owns the theme, the background and the system status and navigation bars.

*Drawn on the screen.*

**Events** — `Initialize`

## Column

A layout container that stacks its children top to bottom. Use it for lists, forms and cards, and style it with a background, border, corners and shadow.

*Drawn on the screen.*

**Events** — `Click`, `Long Click`

## Row

A layout container that lays its children out side by side, left to right. Use it for toolbars, button strips and icon-and-text rows.

*Drawn on the screen.*

**Events** — `Click`, `Long Click`

## Stack

A layout container that layers child components on top of each other along the z-axis. Supports alignment positioning and z-indexing.

*Drawn on the screen.*

**Events** — `Click`, `Long Click`

## ScrollableColumn

A vertical stack of children inside a scrolling area, so content taller than the space available can be scrolled through. No scrollbar is drawn.

*Drawn on the screen.*

**Events** — `Click`, `Long Click`

## ScrollableRow

A horizontal strip of children that scrolls sideways, for carousels and chip rails whose children are wider than the screen. No scrollbar is drawn.

*Drawn on the screen.*

**Events** — `Click`, `Long Click`

## CardView

A tappable container with rounded corners, a border and a drop shadow, used to group related content into a raised card.

*Drawn on the screen.*

**Events** — `Click`, `Long Press`, `Focus`, `Blur`

## TopBar

The bar across the top of a screen, holding a title, an optional subtitle, a navigation icon on the left, and tappable action icons on the right.

*Drawn on the screen.*

**Events** — `Navigation Click`

## BottomNavigation

A bar pinned to the bottom of the screen holding up to five labeled icons, used to switch between the main sections of an app.

*Drawn on the screen.*

**Events** — `Item Selected`

## TabLayout

A row of tabs that pairs itself with a ViewPager on the same screen: swiping moves the tabs, tapping moves the pager. Works on its own too.

*Drawn on the screen.*

**Events** — `Tab Selected`

**Functions** — `Select Tab`, `Tab Count`

## ViewPager

A container whose children each become one full-size page the user swipes between, and which pairs automatically with a TabLayout on the same screen.

*Drawn on the screen.*

**Events** — `Page Changed`

**Functions** — `Next Page`, `Previous Page`, `Go To Page`

## Dialog

A modal container the user fills with any components, shown in its own window over the screen. Used for confirmations, alerts and short forms.

*Drawn on the screen.*

**Events** — `Shown`, `Dismissed`

**Functions** — `is Showing`, `Show`, `Dismiss`

## BottomSheet

A panel that slides up from the bottom of the screen over the current content and holds child widgets. Used for menus, filters and quick actions.

*Drawn on the screen.*

**Events** — `Shown`, `Dismissed`

**Functions** — `is Showing`, `Show`, `Dismiss`

## DynamicLoader

A container that renders a Duddy document your server sends at run time — server-driven UI inside a shipped app. Load a document into it, patch it a commit at a time, and call the logic it declares by name.

*Drawn on the screen.*

**Events** — `Loaded`, `Error Occurred`

**Functions** — `Load`, `Apply`, `Reset`, `Call Block`
