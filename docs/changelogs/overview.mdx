reach the builder as they are deployed, and your projects come with them.

## What "in testing" means

A feature marked **in testing** is in the product for everyone — it is not an invite list.
It works, and it is the part most likely to change while it is hardened. Documentation
here covers what is **live**; pages for features in testing arrive as they settle.

## Your app's own versions

The version numbers that matter for a published app are yours, not Duddy's: the **version
code** and **version name** under
[Project settings](/getting-started/project-settings#build). Raise the version code on every
store upload.

Inside the builder, a project keeps its own history — every change, who made it, and a way
back to any point. See [Collaboration](/guides/collaboration/teamwork#history-and-versions).
