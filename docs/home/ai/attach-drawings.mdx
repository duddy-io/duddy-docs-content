---
title: Attach drawings
description: Sketch a layout or attach a screenshot, and have Vibe mode build the screen you drew.
---

A rough drawing says more about a layout than a paragraph does. Vibe mode takes both:
sketch the screen in the built-in drawing pad, or attach an image you already have, and
the AI builds against it.

## Attach an image

Paste an image straight into the chat box, or press the attachment button beside it and
choose **Attach an Image**.

- Any image format, **under 1 MB**.
- Screenshots, photos of a whiteboard, an exported design, a competitor's screen you want
  the structure of.
- If your message is empty, Duddy fills in a starting prompt for you — replace it with
  something specific and you will get a better result.

<Callout type="info">
  Any model will do, on any plan. If the model you picked cannot see images, Duddy swaps a
  vision-capable one in for that turn and says so on the turn's status line — your pick is
  left alone whenever it can do the job. See [Models](/ai/models).
</Callout>

## Draw it instead

Choose **Create a Drawing** to open a drawing pad inside the chat. Boxes, arrows, text,
freehand — enough to say "search at the top, a grid of cards, a tab bar at the bottom".
Press **Compose** and the sketch is attached to your next message as an image, exported at
a size the model reads once rather than at full resolution.

<Steps>
  <Step>Block out the screen: one rectangle per region, roughly to scale.</Step>
  <Step>Label the parts you care about — "search", "price", "buy button". Labels are read.</Step>
  <Step>Press **Compose**, then write what the screen is for and anything the drawing cannot say.</Step>
</Steps>

## Writing the message that goes with it

The drawing carries structure; your words carry everything else. A sketch plus one good
sentence beats either alone.

| Say | Instead of |
|---|---|
| "Build this as a product screen. The grid is two columns, cards are tappable, the bar at the bottom is navigation." | "Make this." |
| "Same layout, but use our theme colours rather than the greys in the image." | "Fix the colours." |
| "Only the header — leave the rest of the screen as it is." | "Redo the top." |

<Callout type="warning">
  An attachment is context for **one message**. Attach it again if a follow-up turn needs to see
  it too, or describe the change in words instead.
</Callout>

## What it is good at, and what it is not

**Good at:** structure and hierarchy — what sits where, what is a list, what is a bar, what
is emphasised.

**Not a copier:** it will not reproduce a screenshot pixel for pixel, and it should not — the
result is built from real components that resize, theme and behave. If you need exact
values, set them in the properties panel afterwards, or start from a
[Figma design](/integration/figma) instead.
