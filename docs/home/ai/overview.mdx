title: Vibe mode
description: Describe a screen, an edit or a whole app in the chat, and Duddy builds it into your project as ordinary components and blocks.

Vibe mode is the AI chat in the designer. You describe what you want, it builds it — a
section on the screen you are looking at, a set of new screens, the logic behind a button,
or a change to the theme.

What it writes is not a special AI layer. It is the same component tree and the same
blocks you would have made by hand, so every one of them can be selected, dragged,
renamed and rewired afterwards.

## What a turn can do

Duddy decides what kind of turn you asked for and runs the matching pipeline.

| Kind | Example |
|---|---|
| **Answer** | "What does Weight do on a Row?" |
| **Edit** | "Make the card corners rounder and the price bold." |
| **Design** | "Add a filter bar under the title." |
| **New screens** | "Add a checkout screen and an order confirmation screen." |
| **Whole app** | "A recipe app with a browse screen, search, and a saved list." |
| **Logic** | "When Sign in is tapped, validate the email and go to Home." |
| **Project actions** | Renaming a screen, changing the theme, finding or generating an asset. |

If you would rather choose the route yourself, type `/` in the chat and pick a command:

| Command | Does |
|---|---|
| `/edit` | Change something already on screen. |
| `/section` | Design new UI on the current screen. |
| `/screens` | Design one or more new screens. |
| `/logic` | Wire behaviour on components that already exist. |
| `/theme` | Change the whole app's colour scheme. |
| `/asset` | Find, upload or manage assets. |
| `/screen` | Rename or delete a screen. |
| `/settings` | Project settings, or leave a note. |
| `/figma` | Point the AI at one of your [Figma](/integration/figma) designs. |
| `/imagine` | Generate an image straight into your assets. |

## Watching it build

A build turn works screen by screen. For each one it designs the screen first and shows
you that design, then translates it into real components, checks its own work, and wires
the logic last. Screens appear on the canvas as they finish — you do not wait for the
whole app before you can look at anything.

You can keep working while it runs. If you close the tab, the turn carries on.

## Settings

Open the settings icon in the chat header. Five choices, and they are genuinely different
knobs rather than one quality slider:

| Setting | What it changes |
|---|---|
| **Model** | Which AI writes your app. See [Models](/ai/models). |
| **Thinking** | How much the model deliberates before answering — Off, or Low/Medium/High where the model supports it. |
| **Detail** | *Which* stages a build runs. **Reduced** is one pass, **Normal** is a full design pass then build, **Ultra** adds states and edge cases and reviews a render of the finished screen. |
| **Quality** | *How hard* each stage is pushed. **Fast** does the fewest passes, **Balanced** is the default, **Best** adds a design critique and reviews every screen. |
| **UI style** | The look it defaults to. See [UI style](/ai/ui-style). |

<Callout type="info">
  **Ultra** looks at a render of the screen it just built, so it needs a model that can see
  images. Pick a vision model and the option becomes available.
</Callout>

Bigger settings cost more credits and take longer. A wording tweak does not need Ultra and
Best; a first-draft app screen usually deserves them.

## Giving it context

- **Attach an image** — a screenshot, a photo of a whiteboard, a competitor's screen. Under 1 MB.
- **Draw it** — sketch the layout you want in the built-in drawing pad. See
  [Attach drawings](/ai/attach-drawings).
- **Point at a design** — `/figma` attaches one of your Figma files as the reference.

## Sessions

Chats are saved per project. Start a new chat when you move to a different piece of work —
the old conversation stays in the history where you can reopen it, so nothing is thrown
away to get a clean slate.

## When it gets something wrong

Everything an AI turn does arrives as ordinary project history, so you have three ways out:

<Steps>
  <Step>Tell it. "Not that — keep the layout, just change the palette" is usually one cheap turn.</Step>
  <Step>Fix it by hand on the canvas. Nothing it wrote is locked.</Step>
  <Step>Revert the turn from the history panel. The whole turn goes back, including screens it created.</Step>
</Steps>

## What it costs

Every turn spends credits, charged by what the turn actually used — see
[Credits and plans](/getting-started/credits-and-plans). If a turn is refused, the chat
says which of the three reasons it was: not enough credits, a model your plan does not
include, or Vibe being switched off for your plan.
