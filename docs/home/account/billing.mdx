Plans, credits and payments live in the dashboard's Account area, under **Subscription**. Open it from your picture at
the top right of the dashboard: **Upgrade Plan** opens **Account → Plan**, and **Add more credits** opens
**Account → Credits**.

| Page | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Your plan, credits used and left, when the plan renews or ends, and **Cancel subscription**. |
| **Plan** | The plans side by side, the **Monthly** and **Yearly** prices, and the button to switch. |
| **Credits** | Top-ups, and **Credit history**: every credit in and out of your balance. |
| **Usage** | Credits spent per day, your limits this cycle, all credit activity and **Export CSV**. |
| **Billing** | How you pay, your next charge and **Payments**, the history of every charge. |

What each plan includes is on [Plans and limits](/account/plans-and-limits); what uses credits is on
[Credits](/getting-started/credits-and-plans).

## Before you start

- **A phone number on your account.** Checkout needs one for your payment confirmation. If your profile has none, the
  checkout asks for a **Mobile number** and saves it for next time. You can add it in advance under
  **Account → Contact info**.
- **For a top-up, an active paid plan.** The free trial sells no top-ups.

## Buy or switch a plan

<Steps>
  <Step>Open **Account → Plan**.</Step>
  <Step>Choose **Monthly** or **Yearly** at the top. **Yearly** shows how much you save: a year costs ten months' price.</Step>
  <Step>Press **Switch to Starter** or **Switch to Pro** on the plan you want. The checkout opens beside the page.</Step>
  <Step>Check the summary and **Due today**. If you have a promo code, enter it in **Promo code** and press **Apply**.</Step>
  <Step>Press the pay button, for example **Pay $50 and start**. Duddy opens its payment provider's secure checkout, where you choose how to pay.</Step>
  <Step>Complete the payment. Duddy confirms it, either in the checkout or on a **Payment received** page, and the plan's limits apply straight away.</Step>
</Steps>

A few things to know before you switch:

- **You are charged once now, and again at the start of each period** until you cancel. Switching starts a new term on
  the day you pay.
- **Unused credits roll over** while you stay subscribed. Credits left from the free trial do not carry over to a paid
  plan.
- **Moving to a smaller plan needs room for your apps.** If you have more apps than the new plan allows, the checkout
  refuses the switch. Archived projects count, so delete the ones you no longer need first.
- **For Enterprise**, contact Duddy instead: see [Plans and limits](/account/plans-and-limits#enterprise).

## Top up credits

<Steps>
  <Step>Open **Account → Credits**.</Step>
  <Step>Pick one of the five amounts, from $5 to $100. Each tile shows how many credits it adds. Or enter a whole-dollar amount in **Or set your own amount**.</Step>
  <Step>If you have a promo code, enter it in **Promo code** and press **Apply**.</Step>
  <Step>Press the **Add … credits** button, which names the credits you get and the price, then pay in the checkout.</Step>
  <Step>Duddy confirms the payment with **Credits added** or **Payment received**, and the credits are in your balance.</Step>
</Steps>

The smallest top-up is $5 and the largest single top-up is $5,000. On the free trial, **Account → Credits** shows
**Top-ups come with a plan** instead: choose a plan to get more credits.

## How you pay

- **You choose how to pay at checkout, every time.** Duddy never sees or stores your card details, so
  **Account → Billing** lists no cards on file.
- **Prices are in US dollars.** In India, checkout charges the equivalent in rupees. **Billed in** on
  **Account → Billing** shows the currency your charges are taken in.
- **Promo codes** apply to a plan or a top-up. A promo code cannot be used to renew the plan you are already on. If a
  promo price covers only your first term, the checkout says so before you pay, and you buy the plan again when that
  term ends.

## Check your usage and payments

- **Account → Usage** charts the credits you spent each day, shows **Limits this cycle** (plan credits, projects,
  builds this hour and top-up credits), and lists **All credit activity** by date, feature and project. Press
  **Export CSV** to download it.
- **Account → Billing** shows your **Next charge** and **Payments**, every charge with its date, description, method,
  amount and status. Filter it by **Subscriptions** or **Top-ups**.

| Payment status | Means |
|---|---|
| **Created** | A checkout was started. |
| **Pending** | Waiting for the payment provider to confirm the payment. |
| **Paid** | The payment cleared. |
| **Failed** | The payment did not complete, or Duddy could not apply it. If money left your account, email support@duddy.io. |
| **Refunded** | The payment was refunded. |

## Cancel or resume a plan

Cancelling stops the next charge. It does not end your plan early: you keep everything until the end of the term you
paid for.

<Steps>
  <Step>Open **Account → Overview**.</Step>
  <Step>In the **Current plan** card, press **Cancel subscription**.</Step>
  <Step>Press **Cancel plan** to confirm. Duddy confirms the date your plan stays active until.</Step>
</Steps>

To undo a cancellation, press **Resume subscription** in the same card before the plan ends and confirm with
**Resume**. The plan then renews as usual.

- **The free trial has nothing to cancel.** It ends on its own and charges nothing.
- **A plan bought with a one-term promo price does not renew**, so it has no **Cancel subscription** button.
- **When a plan ends**, the designer asks you to renew before it opens projects again. Your projects stay in your
  account; see [Plans and limits](/account/plans-and-limits#when-the-trial-or-a-plan-ends).

## Refunds

Refunds follow the [Duddy Refund Policy](https://duddy.io/refund-policy). Plans and credits are generally not
refundable, but billing mistakes, such as a duplicate charge or a charge after you cancelled, are. To ask about a
charge, email support@duddy.io from the address on your account within 30 days of the charge, with the date, the amount
and the payment reference.

## Troubleshooting

<Accordion>
  <AccordionItem value="no-plan" title="You need an active plan before you can buy credits.">
    **Why:** credits are added to an active plan, and you have none.

    **Fix:** press **Buy a plan** and choose a plan first, then top up.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem value="trial-topup" title="Top-ups come with a plan">
    **Why:** you are on the free trial, whose credits are a fixed allowance.

    **Fix:** press **Choose a plan**. A paid plan adds its own credits and lets you top up.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem value="downgrade" title="cannot downgrade: you have 7 active apps, but starter allows only 5. Delete apps first.">
    **Why:** you have more apps than the smaller plan allows. Archived projects count towards the limit.

    **Fix:** delete projects you no longer need until you are within the new plan's limit, then switch again.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem value="phone" title="Add a mobile number to continue.">
    **Why:** checkout needs a phone number for your payment confirmation.

    **Fix:** enter it in **Mobile number** in the checkout. It is saved to your profile.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem value="renewal-promo" title="promo codes cannot be applied to a renewal">
    **Why:** you chose the plan you are already on, and a promo code cannot renew it.

    **Fix:** remove the code, or use it on a different plan or a top-up.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem value="amount" title="Minimum purchase is $5">
    **Why:** a top-up must be at least $5, in whole dollars. A decimal amount shows **Whole dollars only**.

    **Fix:** enter a whole-dollar amount of $5 or more, or pick a tile.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem value="confirming" title="We're confirming this with your bank. Your account updates automatically — this usually takes under a minute.">
    **Why:** the payment went through, and the provider has not confirmed it yet.

    **Fix:** wait a minute and reload the page. The payment shows as **Pending** until it clears. If it stays that way,
    email support@duddy.io with the payment reference.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem value="failed" title="That payment didn't go through">
    **Why:** the payment provider declined or cancelled the payment. The page says "Nothing has been charged."

    **Fix:** press **Try again**, and pick a different payment method at the checkout if it fails again.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem value="closed" title="Checkout closed. Nothing has been charged.">
    **Why:** the payment window was closed before you paid.

    **Fix:** start again from **Account → Plan** or **Account → Credits**.
  </AccordionItem>
</Accordion>
