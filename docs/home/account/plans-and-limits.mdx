Every plan, the free trial included, has the whole designer: the canvas, logic blocks, Vibe mode and Live test. What a
plan changes is how many credits you get and how much you can run through the designer, plus a few features such as
iOS builds and the larger AI models.

Prices on this page are in US dollars. The current prices, and the details of each plan, are always on
[duddy.io/pricing](https://duddy.io/pricing).

## Compare plans

| Included | Free trial | Starter | Pro |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Price** | Free for 7 days, no card needed | $30 a month, or $300 a year | $50 a month, or $500 a year |
| **Plan credits** | 100, once | 300 | 1,000 |
| **Daily AI credit limit** | 40 on day one, then 10 a day | None | None |
| **Apps (projects)** | 2 | 5 | 50 |
| **Versions per project** | 1 | 3 | 7 |
| **Collaborators per project** | 5 | None | 5 |
| **Free builds an hour** | 1 | 1 | 5 |
| **Each build past the hourly limit** | 7 credits | 7 credits | 3 credits |
| **Android builds (`.apk`)** | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **iOS builds** <Badge type="beta" /> | Yes | No | Yes |
| **AI model tiers** | Free | Free and Standard | Free, Standard and Premium |
| **Paid marketplace components** | Yes | No | Yes |
| **Asset storage per project version** | 10 MB | 10 MB | 50 MB |
| **Credit top-ups** | Not sold | Yes | Yes |
| **Duddy splash and "Made with Duddy" badge in built apps** | Yes | No | No |

A few rules behind the numbers:

- **Plan credits on monthly billing** are added when the plan starts and each time it renews. Unused credits roll over
  while you stay subscribed. How credits are spent is explained in [Credits](/getting-started/credits-and-plans).
- **Archived projects count** towards your app limit. Deleted projects do not.
- **Free builds are counted per account**, across all your projects. Live test builds have their own allowance and
  never count towards it; see [Live test](/build/live-test).
- **Web builds and hosting are coming soon** on every plan.

## The free trial

Every new account starts on the free trial. It needs no card, and nothing is charged when it ends.

- **7 days and 100 credits**, spent at a steady pace: up to 40 on your first day, then up to 10 on each day after. A
  day is 24 hours from when your trial started. See
  [The free trial's daily limit](/getting-started/credits-and-plans#the-free-trials-daily-limit).
- **Free-tier AI models only.** Standard models need a paid plan and Premium models need Pro; the model picker
  shows them locked. See [Models](/ai/models).
- **iOS builds and paid marketplace components are included**, so you can try them before you choose a plan.
- **Apps you build carry Duddy branding**: a Duddy splash when the app opens and a "Made with Duddy" badge. Branding is
  decided when the build starts, so build again after you upgrade to get an app without it.
- **No top-ups.** For more credits, choose a plan.

The dashboard header shows **Free Trial** with the days left; select it to compare plans.

## When the trial or a plan ends

When your trial or plan ends, the designer stops opening projects and shows **Subscription Plan expired**: "Your plan
has expired. Please renew your subscription to continue using duddy." Press **Renew Plan** to go to **Account → Plan**
and choose a plan.

- **Your projects stay in your account.** Buy a plan and they open in the designer again.
- **Unused trial credits do not carry over** to a paid plan. The paid plan starts with its own credits.

To buy, switch or cancel a plan, see [Billing](/account/billing).

## What you see at a limit

Each limit is checked when you try the action, and the message names the limit you reached.

| Limit | What you see | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Apps** | "You've reached your plan's app limit (2/2). Upgrade your plan to create more apps." | Delete a project you no longer need (archived ones still count), or upgrade. |
| **Versions** | "Version limit reached for your plan (3/3 used)" | Delete a version you no longer need, or upgrade. See [History and versions](/guides/collaboration/teamwork#history-and-versions). |
| **Collaborators** | "Collaborator limit reached for your plan" | Starter includes no collaborators, so move to Pro to invite people. See [Collaboration](/guides/collaboration/teamwork). |
| **Asset storage** | "Asset storage limit reached for your plan (10/10 used)" | Delete assets you no longer use, or upgrade. |
| **Builds an hour** | **This build costs credits** | Press **Build** to pay, or wait for the hour to end. See [Builds and your hourly limit](/getting-started/credits-and-plans#builds-and-your-hourly-limit). |
| **iOS builds** | "iOS builds are not included in your plan" | Move to Pro. See [iOS builds](/build/ios-builds). |
| **Paid marketplace components** | "Paid marketplace components aren't included in your plan. Upgrade to import premium components." | Move to Pro, or use a free component. See [Import components](/guides/marketplace/import-components). |
| **AI models** | "(model name) isn't in your plan", or a lock with "Standard models come with a paid plan." | Pick a model your plan includes, or upgrade. See [Models](/ai/models). |
| **No active plan** | "You don't have an active plan, so you can't create an app yet." | Choose a plan in **Account → Plan**. |

If Duddy cannot check your plan for a moment, you see "We can't verify your plan right now. Please try again in a
moment." or a message that billing is temporarily unreachable. Nothing is charged; try again shortly, and check
[status.duddy.io](https://status.duddy.io) if it continues.

## Enterprise

Enterprise has custom limits and terms, agreed with you rather than picked from a list. To ask about one, use
[the Duddy contact page](https://duddy.io/contact-us). On Enterprise, **Account → Plan** shows your plan instead of
the plan list, with "Your limits are agreed with us rather than picked from a list. Talk to us to change them."
