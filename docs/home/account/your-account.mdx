One Duddy account signs you in to the dashboard at [app.duddy.io](https://app.duddy.io) and to the designer. Signing
in happens on Duddy's sign-in page, and everything about your account, from your profile to your plan, is in the
dashboard's Account area. Open it from your picture at the top right of the dashboard.

Your plan, credits and payments are covered in [Billing](/account/billing). This page covers the rest.

## Create an account

You can sign up with your email address, Google or GitHub. Every new account starts on the free trial, with no card
needed: see [Plans and limits](/account/plans-and-limits#the-free-trial).

To sign up with Google or GitHub, press **Google** or **GitHub** on the sign-in page and approve the request. To sign
up with your email address:

<Steps>
  <Step>On the sign-in page, press **New here? Create an account**.</Step>
  <Step>Enter your full name, at least 3 characters of letters and spaces, then your email address.</Step>
  <Step>Duddy shows **Email verification required** and emails you an activation link. Open the email.</Step>
  <Step>Select the link, then set and confirm your password. Your account is ready.</Step>
</Steps>

A password needs at least 8 characters, with an upper-case letter, a lower-case letter, a number and a special
character such as `!` or `#`. **Suggest a strong password** fills one in for you.

## Sign in

On the sign-in page, choose **Email** (with your password), **Google** or **GitHub**. Email sign-in asks for your email
address, then your password.

If you forget your password, press **Forgot your password? Reset Now** on the password step, enter your email address
and follow the link in the email Duddy sends you to set a new one.

To sign out, open the menu under your picture in the dashboard and press **Signout**.

## Turn on email and password login

If you signed up with Google or GitHub, email and password login is off, and signing in with your email address shows
**Email login is not enabled**. To turn it on:

<Steps>
  <Step>Sign in with Google or GitHub, and open **Account → Login & email**.</Step>
  <Step>Turn on **Email and password login**.</Step>
  <Step>Enter a **New password** and type it again in **Confirm new password**. If the account had a password before, Duddy also asks for your **Current password**.</Step>
  <Step>Press **Turn on**. You can now sign in with your email address and that password.</Step>
</Steps>

Turning the switch off again asks you to confirm with **Disable**.

## Change your password or email

- **Password:** in **Account → Login & email**, fill in **Current password**, **New password** and
  **Confirm new password** in the **Password** card, then press **Update password**. The card appears only while email
  and password login is on.
- **Email address:** you cannot change it yourself. Contact support, as the **Change email** button explains.

## Edit your profile

Your name and picture appear beside the apps you share and in the collaborator lists of projects you join.

| Page | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Profile** | Your picture. A JPG, PNG, WebP or GIF up to 4 MB uploads as it is; a larger photo is resized for you. |
| **Personal info** | **Full name**, **Username**, **Bio**, **Company** and **Role**. You can change your username once, using lower-case letters, numbers and underscores. |
| **Social profiles** | Links shown beside your name, such as **Website**, **GitHub** and **LinkedIn**. Leave a field empty to remove it. |
| **Contact info** | Your **Account email**, **Phone number** and **WhatsApp number**. Checkout needs a phone number; see [Billing](/account/billing#before-you-start). |
| **Address** | Your address. It is kept with your account and nothing is posted to it. |

## Language, theme and notifications

- **Language:** in **Account → Language**, choose English, Hindi, Spanish or Chinese. It applies everywhere in Duddy,
  including the designer. Languages marked **Not translated** are not available yet.
- **Theme:** in **Account → Theme**, pick a look. It changes the page straight away and applies in this browser, in
  the designer as well as the dashboard.
- **Notifications:** in **Account → Notifications**, choose what reaches you. The bell at the top of the dashboard
  opens the same page.

| Setting | What it sends |
|---|---|
| **Desktop notifications** | A notice in this browser when a build finishes, plus updates and offers. |
| **Important updates** | Projects, builds and changes that affect what you have already made. |
| **Product updates and offers** | New features and the occasional offer, about once a month. |
| **Security and account email** | Sign-ins, password changes and receipts. Always on. |

## Invite friends

**Account → Invite friends** holds your invite link. Press **Copy**, or send it straight to another app from
**Share it**. Anyone who signs up through your link counts towards your rewards, and **Open affiliate dashboard** shows
your referrals and payouts.

## Delete your account

There is no delete button in the dashboard. To delete your account and its data, email support@duddy.io from the
address on your account. The [Privacy Policy](https://duddy.io/privacy-policy) explains what is deleted and when.

## Troubleshooting

<Accordion>
  <AccordionItem value="region" title="Duddy isn't available in your country yet.">
    **Why:** Duddy is not taking new sign-ups where you are right now.

    **Fix:** you cannot create an account from there yet. If you already have an account, you can still sign in.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem value="email-login" title="Email login is not enabled">
    **Why:** you signed up with Google or GitHub, so the account has no email and password login.

    **Fix:** sign in with Google or GitHub, then [turn on email and password login](#turn-on-email-and-password-login).
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem value="not-found" title="Account not found">
    **Why:** no account uses that email address.

    **Fix:** check the address for typos, try Google or GitHub if you signed up that way, or create an account.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem value="already" title="You've already signed up!">
    **Why:** an account already uses that email address.

    **Fix:** press **Sign in**, or reset your password if you have forgotten it.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem value="token" title="Invalid or Expired Token">
    **Why:** the link in your email is no longer valid, perhaps because you used it already or asked for a newer one.

    **Fix:** press **Reset Password** and request a new email.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem value="locked" title="Your account is temporarily locked">
    **Why:** too many sign-in attempts, or a security issue, locked the account.

    **Fix:** try again later, or press **Contact Support** to regain access.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem value="oauth" title="Google authentication failed">
    **Why:** Google did not complete the sign-in. GitHub shows **GitHub authentication failed** for the same reason.

    **Fix:** press **Retry** and approve the request again.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem value="password-rules" title="Password must contain at least one special character">
    **Why:** the password is missing one of the required kinds of character. Similar messages ask for 8 characters, an
    upper-case letter, a lower-case letter or a number.

    **Fix:** add what the message asks for, or press **Suggest a strong password**.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem value="blocked" title="Your account has been blocked">
    **Why:** the account was blocked because of unusual activity or a policy violation.

    **Fix:** press **Contact Support** if you think this is a mistake.
  </AccordionItem>
</Accordion>
