title: Credits and plans
description: One balance covers AI, builds and hosting — how credits are spent, what a plan changes, and how to keep a project cheap.

Duddy meters the things that cost real money to run — AI generation, compiling an app, and
keeping a web app online — against a single **credit balance**. Everything else in the
builder is included in your plan.

One number to watch, and every charge itemised in your account.

## What spends credits

| Action | Charged |
|---|---|
| **A Vibe turn** | Per turn, by what the turn actually used. A one-line edit costs a fraction of what building a whole app costs. |
| **Generating an image** | Per image, when you use `/imagine` or generate from the asset picker. |
| **A build past your hourly limit** | A few credits per build — builds within your plan's limit are free. See [Builds and your hourly limit](#builds-and-your-hourly-limit). |
| **Hosting** | Per period while a web app is online, at the rate shown on the Hosting page. |

Designing on the canvas, wiring blocks, collaborating, previewing, uploading assets,
saving versions and building within your plan's hourly limit cost nothing.

## What a plan changes

A turn costs the same on every plan. What a plan changes is **how many credits your money
buys**, plus the limits around them: how many builds you can run in an hour, whether there is
a daily limit on spending, whether iOS builds are available, and which AI models you can select.

The plan ladder and current prices live in your dashboard under **Account → Plan**.

## Builds and your hourly limit

**Builds within your plan's hourly build limit are free.** Past that limit a build isn't
refused: it costs a few credits from your balance — **7 on the free trial and Starter, 3 on Pro
and Enterprise**.

- **You're asked first.** When you start a build past your limit, the builder shows the price
  and asks before charging anything. If you cancel, nothing is spent.
- **It's the same balance.** Build credits come out of the balance your AI generations use, and
  count towards your daily credit limit if your plan has one — so a build your available
  credits can't cover isn't started.
- **You're charged once the build is queued**, and the charge shows in your credit history as
  **Build overage**.
- **Live Test builds are separate.** They have their own rebuild allowance and never cost
  overage credits.

<Callout type="info" title="The trial is a trial, not a free tier">
  A new account starts on a seven-day trial with 100 credits: 40 on your first day, then 10 a
  day. It is enough to design a real app and build it a few times — it is not a permanent free plan.
</Callout>

## The trial's daily limit

The trial's 100 credits are yours from the start, but you spend them at a steady pace:
**up to 40 on your first day, then up to 10 on each day after**. Seven days of that is exactly
the 100, so the whole pool is reachable — just not in one sitting.

- **A day is 24 hours from when your trial started**, not a calendar date. Start at 11 pm and
  your first day runs until 11 pm the next evening. Your dashboard shows how much of today's
  limit is left and when the next day begins, or on your last day when the trial ends. Vibe
  mode tells you the time if it stops you.
- **Nothing is added each day.** The limit decides how much of your balance you can use today;
  it never adds to it, and what you leave unspent does not carry over to raise tomorrow's limit.
- **The trial sells no top-ups.** Its 100 credits are a fixed allowance — for more, choose a
  plan, which adds credits every month and can be topped up whenever you need.
- **Paid plans have no daily limit** unless the plan says otherwise. Your balance is the only
  ceiling, and where a plan does set a daily limit, top-ups raise your balance, not the day's
  limit.

## Reading your balance

**Account → Credits** shows what you have, what you have used, and a ledger of every charge
with the action that caused it. The Vibe chat also shows your balance in its header, and
updates it the moment a turn settles.

Two things worth knowing:

- **A turn needs a small minimum balance to start.** A long turn makes many model calls, and
  the last one always overshoots slightly, so Duddy refuses to begin a turn it might not be
  able to finish rather than cutting one off halfway.
- **Credits are bought against an active paid plan.** Top-ups are for topping up — a purchase
  with no active plan, or on the free trial, is refused up front rather than taken and stranded.

## Keeping a project cheap

<Steps>
  <Step>**Say more in the first prompt.** One well-described screen costs far less than five rounds of "not like that".</Step>
  <Step>**Use the smaller settings for small work.** [Quality and detail](/ai/overview#settings) are separate knobs, and a text tweak does not need the expensive ones.</Step>
  <Step>**Pick the model to fit the job.** See [Models](/ai/models) — the free-tier models handle edits and questions perfectly well.</Step>
  <Step>**Edit by hand when it is faster.** Changing a colour on the canvas is free; asking for it is not.</Step>
  <Step>**Stop hosting you are not using.** Your files and link are kept, so you can start it again later at the same address.</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning">
  If Vibe mode says it is unavailable rather than offering an upgrade, that is a plan-level
  switch and buying credits will not lift it — check **Account → Plan**.
</Callout>
