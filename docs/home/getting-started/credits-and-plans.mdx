Duddy meters the work that costs real money to run, such as AI generation and builds past your hourly limit, against
one **credit balance**. Designing on the canvas, wiring logic and testing on your own phone are part of every plan and
cost nothing.

How many credits you start with, and the limits around them, depend on your plan: see
[Plans and limits](/account/plans-and-limits). To buy a plan or top up, see [Billing](/account/billing).

## What uses credits

Every charge comes out of the same balance, and your credit history names the feature that caused it.

| Action | Cost | In your credit history |
|---|---|---|
| **A Vibe mode turn** | Charged per turn, for what the turn actually used. A one-line edit costs a small fraction of building a whole app. | **Vibe** |
| **Pictures a build turn makes** | Counted inside that turn. A turn makes them only when **Generate images with AI** is on in **Settings → Project → Build**; it is off by default, and a turn makes at most 6. | **Vibe** |
| **Generating an image yourself** | Charged per image, from `/imagine` in the chat or the **Imagine** tab of the asset picker. | **Imagine** |
| **Generating a palette** | Charged per palette, when you press **Generate with AI** in **Settings → Theme**. | **Imagine-theme** |
| **A build past your hourly limit** | A fixed price set by your plan. Builds within the limit are free. | **Build overage** |
| **Hosting a web app** | 1 credit for every 2 hours online. Hosting arrives with web builds, which are coming soon. | **Web hosting** |

Everything else is free: designing on the canvas, logic blocks, collaborating, previewing,
[Live test](/build/live-test), uploading assets, saving versions and every build within your hourly limit.

## Builds and your hourly limit

Each plan includes a number of free builds an hour. A build past that number is not refused: it costs a fixed number
of credits, listed for each plan in [Plans and limits](/account/plans-and-limits).

- **The limit is per account, not per project.** Every release build you start counts, whichever project it is for.
- **The hour starts with your first build.** It runs from the first build after your last hour ended, not from the
  start of a clock hour.
- **You are asked first.** A build past the limit opens **This build costs credits** with the price, for example
  "You've used this hour's builds. This build costs 7 credits." Press **Build** to spend the credits, or cancel and
  nothing is charged.
- **It is the same balance.** A build that your balance cannot cover is not started. The credits are taken when the
  build is queued and show in your history as **Build overage**.
- **Live test builds are separate.** Building or rebuilding a test app never counts towards this limit and never costs
  credits. It has its own allowance, explained on [Live test](/build/live-test).

## The free trial's daily limit

The free trial's 100 credits are yours from the start, but you spend them at a steady pace: **up to 40 on your first
day, then up to 10 on each day after**. Seven days of that is exactly 100, so you can use every credit, though not in one
sitting.

- **A day is 24 hours from when your trial started**, not a calendar date. Start at 11 pm and your first day runs until
  11 pm the next evening.
- **Nothing is added each day.** The limit decides how much of your balance you can use today. It never adds to the
  balance, and credits you leave unspent do not raise tomorrow's limit.
- **You can see where you stand.** **Account → Overview** shows **AI credits today** and when more arrive, and Vibe
  mode tells you when your next credits arrive if it stops you.
- **Paid plans have no daily limit.** Your balance is the only ceiling.

The trial sells no top-ups. For more credits, choose a plan: see [Plans and limits](/account/plans-and-limits).

## Check your balance and history

Your balance lives in the dashboard's Account area:

- **Account → Overview** shows how many credits you have used, how many are left and when your plan or trial ends.
- **Account → Credits** ends with **Credit history**, every credit in and out of your balance. **See everything** opens
  **Account → Usage**, where you can also export your activity as a CSV file.

The Vibe mode chat does not show your balance, but its header shows a badge when credits are about to stop a turn.
Hover over a badge to read the details.

| Badge | Means |
|---|---|
| **Credits low** | Your balance is under 50 credits. |
| **Credits too low** | Your balance is below what a new turn needs to start. |
| **Today's credits used** | The free trial's daily limit is reached. The details say when more arrive. |
| **Credits in use** | Turns still running on your account are holding the credits a new turn needs. |

A turn holds credits while it runs, so two turns running at once, such as an edit in one project while an AI build
runs in another, can never spend the same credit.

- **A turn needs a minimum balance to start:** 10 credits on a paid plan, 1 on the free trial. Credits held by other
  running turns do not count towards it.
- **A turn that has started always finishes**, even if it empties your balance. The last model call can go past what
  was left, so your balance can drop below zero. The next credits you add clear that first.

## Buy more credits

On a paid plan you can top up whenever you need more, from **Account → Credits**. The steps are in
[Billing](/account/billing). The free trial sells no top-ups; choose a plan instead.

## When you run out: messages and fixes

These are the messages Vibe mode and the designer show when credits stop a request.

<Accordion>
  <AccordionItem value="out-of-credits" title="You're out of AI credits">
    **Why:** your balance is below the minimum a turn needs to start: 10 credits, or 1 on the trial.

    **Fix:** press **Top up** on the card, or move to a bigger plan. On the trial the card offers **See plans** instead.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem value="overdrawn" title="Your AI credits are overdrawn">
    **Why:** the last turn finished after your balance ran out, so the balance is below zero.

    **Fix:** top up to clear it and keep building. On the trial, choose a plan.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem value="daily-limit" title="You've used today's AI credits">
    **Why:** the free trial's daily limit is reached. The message says when the next credits arrive.

    **Fix:** wait for your next day, or press **See plans** and choose a plan, which has no daily limit.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem value="held" title="Another request is using your AI credits">
    **Why:** turns still running on your account, perhaps in another project or a collaborator's chat, hold the credits
    this one needs.

    **Fix:** wait for them to finish, then send your message again. The held credits come back as soon as they do.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem value="model-tier" title="(model name) isn't in your plan">
    **Why:** the turn uses a Standard or Premium model and your plan does not include that tier. The card says, for
    example, "It's a standard model. Switch to a free model to keep going, or upgrade to unlock it."

    **Fix:** press **Switch model** and pick a model your plan includes, or press **See plans** to upgrade. See
    [Models](/ai/models).
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem value="build-overage" title="This build costs credits">
    **Why:** you have used your plan's free builds for this hour.

    **Fix:** press **Build** to pay the price shown, or wait until the hour is up. Keep testing with
    [Live test](/build/live-test) meanwhile; it is free.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem value="vibe-unavailable" title="Vibe Mode is unavailable">
    **Why:** Vibe mode is paused for your plan for now. It is not a plan limit, so buying credits or upgrading does not
    change it.

    **Fix:** try again later, and check [status.duddy.io](https://status.duddy.io) for an incident.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem value="billing-down" title="Billing is temporarily unreachable">
    **Why:** Duddy could not read your balance, and it does not start a turn it cannot charge for.

    **Fix:** try again in a moment. Nothing was charged.
  </AccordionItem>
</Accordion>

## Keep a project cheap

- **Say more in the first prompt.** One well-described screen costs far less than five rounds of "not like that".
- **Turn Quality down for small work.** **Quality** is the one dial that trades time and credits for care, and a text
  tweak does not need **Best**. See [Vibe mode](/ai/overview).
- **Choose the model for the job.** Models are charged by what they use, so a cheaper model for edits and questions
  saves credits. See [Models](/ai/models).
- **Edit by hand when it is faster.** Changing a colour on the canvas is free; asking for it is not.
- **Leave AI images off unless you want them.** With **Generate images with AI** off, a build turn draws a coloured
  tile instead of making a picture.
- **Test on your phone before you build.** [Live test](/build/live-test) is free and follows every edit; a release
  build past your hourly limit is not.
