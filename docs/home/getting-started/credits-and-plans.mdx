title: Credits and plans
description: One balance covers AI, builds and hosting — how credits are spent, what a plan changes, and how to keep a project cheap.
---

Duddy meters the things that cost real money to run — AI generation, compiling an app, and
keeping a web app online — against a single **credit balance**. Everything else in the
builder is included in your plan.

One number to watch, and every charge itemised in your account.

## What spends credits

| Action | Charged |
|---|---|
| **A Vibe turn** | Per turn, by what the turn actually used. A one-line edit costs a fraction of what building a whole app costs. |
| **Generating an image** | Per image, when you use `/imagine` or generate from the asset picker. |
| **A build** | Per build, per target. |
| **Hosting** | Per period while a web app is online, at the rate shown on the Hosting page. |

Designing on the canvas, wiring blocks, collaborating, previewing, uploading assets and
saving versions cost nothing.

## What a plan changes

A turn costs the same on every plan. What a plan changes is **how many credits your money
buys**, plus the limits around them: how many builds you can run in an hour, whether iOS
builds are available, and which AI models you can select.

The plan ladder and current prices live in your dashboard under **Account → Plan**.

<Callout type="info" title="The trial is a trial, not a free tier">
  A new account starts on a seven-day trial with a pool of 100 credits and a daily ceiling.
  It is enough to design a real app and build it a few times — it is not a permanent free plan.
</Callout>

## Reading your balance

**Account → Credits** shows what you have, what you have used, and a ledger of every charge
with the action that caused it. The Vibe chat also shows your balance in its header, and
updates it the moment a turn settles.

Two things worth knowing:

- **A turn needs a small minimum balance to start.** A long turn makes many model calls, and
  the last one always overshoots slightly, so Duddy refuses to begin a turn it might not be
  able to finish rather than cutting one off halfway.
- **Credits are bought against an active plan.** Top-ups are for topping up — a purchase with
  no active plan is refused up front rather than taken and stranded.

## Keeping a project cheap

<Steps>
  <Step>**Say more in the first prompt.** One well-described screen costs far less than five rounds of "not like that".</Step>
  <Step>**Use the smaller settings for small work.** [Quality and detail](/ai/overview#quality-and-detail) are separate knobs, and a text tweak does not need the expensive ones.</Step>
  <Step>**Pick the model to fit the job.** See [Models](/ai/models) — the free-tier models handle edits and questions perfectly well.</Step>
  <Step>**Edit by hand when it is faster.** Changing a colour on the canvas is free; asking for it is not.</Step>
  <Step>**Stop hosting you are not using.** Your files and link are kept, so you can start it again later at the same address.</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning">
  If Vibe mode says it is unavailable rather than offering an upgrade, that is a plan-level
  switch and buying credits will not lift it — check **Account → Plan**.
</Callout>
