Assets are the files your app ships with. They live with the project, are available on
every screen, and are compiled into the app when you build.

Open the assets area with `6`.

## The three kinds

| Kind | Holds | Used by |
|---|---|---|
| **Drawables** | Images — PNG, JPG, SVG, WebP. | `Image`, backgrounds, icons, the launcher icon and splash. |
| **Fonts** | Font families you upload. | Every Typography row in the properties panel. |
| **Raw** | Anything else — JSON, Lottie animations, audio, small data files. | `Lottie`, `AudioPlayer`, and your logic. |

<Callout type="warning">
  Each file has to be **under 1 MB**. That is a real constraint on app size, not an arbitrary one:
  every asset ships inside the app on every device that installs it.
</Callout>

## Adding an asset

<Steps>
  <Step>Open the assets area and choose the kind of asset you are adding.</Step>
  <Step>Upload from your machine, or pull one in from a connected source such as Pixabay.</Step>
  <Step>Use it: pick it in an `Image` component's property, or in a background, or by name from your logic.</Step>
</Steps>

Replacing a file keeps its name, so every component already pointing at it picks up the new
version — the usual way to swap an icon set or a photo without touching any screens.

## Generating an image

Nothing in the library fits? Generate one:

- In the asset picker, use **Generate with AI**.
- Or in the [Vibe chat](/ai/overview), `/imagine a flat illustration of a delivery scooter,
  cream background`.

Styles on offer include realistic, 2D vector illustration, 3D illustration, cartoonish,
anime, realistic portrait, fantasy, cyberpunk and pixel art. Generated images land straight
in your drawables and are used like any other asset. Generation spends
[credits](/getting-started/credits-and-plans).

## Photography

With [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/oauth/applications) or Pixabay connected in your
dashboard, the AI can fill generated screens with real photographs rather than grey boxes,
and the asset picker gains a search over those libraries. Everything offered is licensed
for commercial use.

<Callout type="info">
  When no source has a photograph of your subject, Duddy uses a colour block built from your own
  theme rather than an unrelated stock photo. It is a placeholder that says so.
</Callout>

## Naming

Asset names become resource names in the built app. Stick to lowercase letters, numbers and
underscores — `hero_banner.png`, not `Hero Banner (final) v2.png` — and you will never have
to think about it again.
