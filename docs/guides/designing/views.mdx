A *view* is a ready-made piece of a screen, such as a sign-in form or a product card, built from ordinary components and published to the Duddy library by other builders. Your project keeps the views you import in the **Views** panel: press `4`, or choose **Views** on the left rail.

<Callout type="info" title="Coming soon">
  Views are in development. You can find views and import them into your project today. A public gallery for browsing published views, and placing a view on a screen from the **Views** panel, are coming soon.
</Callout>

## Import a view

<Steps>
  <Step>Press `4` to open the **Views** panel.</Step>
  <Step>Press **Explore Views**, or **Gallery** once the panel already holds views. The **Add views** dialog opens.</Step>
  <Step>Type in the search box to find views by name or tag, or by author with `@` and their name.</Step>
  <Step>In the dialog's list, which is its default layout, press **Import** on the view's row. The message "*name* added to your palette" confirms it.</Step>
</Steps>

The view now has a card under **Private** in the **Views** panel, with its name, its author as `@author/name`, and how many times it has been downloaded. Use the search box at the top of the panel to find one when you have several.

## Remove a view

<Steps>
  <Step>In the **Views** panel, press **Delete** on the view's card.</Step>
  <Step>Confirm **Remove this view?** The view leaves your palette. Screens already using it are not affected, and you can import it again from the library.</Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

<Accordion>
  <AccordionItem value="no-views" title="No views found">
    **Why:** nothing in the library matches your search or the filter you chose.

    **Fix:** try fewer or different words, search for an author with `@`, or choose the **All** filter.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem value="error-getting" title="Error getting views">
    **Why:** the designer could not reach the library.

    **Fix:** press **Retry**. If it keeps happening, check your connection and try again in a moment.
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem value="drag-nothing" title="I dragged a view onto the canvas and nothing was added">
    **Why:** placing a view on a screen from the **Views** panel is not available yet.

    **Fix:** build the section from components on the canvas, or describe it to [Vibe mode](/ai/overview) and let it build the section for you.
  </AccordionItem>
</Accordion>
